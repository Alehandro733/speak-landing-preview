GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers.

Do I need to sign in to start?

You can try available exercises as a guest. Sign in to create your own practice.

Is this a full language course?

Not yet. Speak focuses on speaking practice, alongside what you already study.

Does it evaluate my accent?

It checks expected words in recognized speech, not your overall accent or fluency.

Do I need an app?

No installation required. Practice in a supported browser with microphone permission.
