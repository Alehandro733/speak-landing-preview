The method

Less tapping. More talking.

Speak is built around active recall: instead of choosing an answer on screen, you try to bring a phrase back from memory and say it out loud.

No. You can start practicing available library exercises as a guest. Creating your own AI exercise topics or managing personal phrase sets requires signing in.

Speak currently supports English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, and Romanian. You choose both your native language and the language you want to practice.

Not yet. Speak is currently a focused speaking-practice tool, best used alongside the lessons, videos, or other materials you're already learning from. A structured learning path is not part of this version.

Speech recognition looks for the expected words in what you say. Correct matches are revealed as you speak, while hints and replay help with tricky parts. It's not an overall accent or fluency score.

No download is required to start in a supported browser. For voice recognition you need a working microphone and permission to use it. Available speech and playback options vary by device and browser.
